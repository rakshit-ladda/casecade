# AttentionTwin demo guide

This is a two-minute walkthrough of the user-facing phone app and its on-device sample data. Do not claim that real-device delivery or personal ML has been validated until the physical-device checklist passes.

1. Open Today. Show the focus durations and explain that AttentionTwin only acts on apps the user chooses.
2. In Settings, show that apps default to **Allow**. Set a test app to **Block in focus**, begin a session, and send one of the safe sample alerts from Live.
3. Show the decision and its plain-language reason in Live or Phone check-up. Explain that Android posts the notification before AttentionTwin can snooze it; DND can silence the alert when explicitly enabled and granted access.
4. Tap **Pause AttentionTwin**. Explain that this turns off blocking and restores any DND filter the app owns. Use **Release everything now** to see the honest note about Android-controlled snooze timing.
5. Open Insights and load the labeled 14-day demo data. Say clearly that it is synthetic example activity, not this person’s history or a validated attention model.

The app's phone-side notification history and rules work without a laptop backend. The old Lab screens remain available for the backend/research flows.
